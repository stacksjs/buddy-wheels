# Go-to-market plan

November 7, 2026. Proposal for an early-stage hardware business. No campaigns, outreach, paid spending or schedules have been activated. No contacts were messaged.

## Strategic frame and current state

The problem is serious and recurring within an affected household; purchase frequency is low. Build a useful education/referral channel and earn sufficient first-order contribution. Recurring subscription metrics do not describe this product. Current revenue, paid customers, measured CAC, retention and referral performance are all unknown or zero at prelaunch; no invented baseline is used.

The founder owns strategy and product accountability. A rehabilitation advisor and mechanical/testing lead are needed before the product can support commercial claims. A contractor can later help with qualified technical photography and editing. There is no existing marketing team, advertising budget, agency retainer or funding round in the evidence.

## Acquisition: earn the right audience

Start with 20 caregiver and 8 professional discovery interviews. Proposed owner: founder. Mechanism: expose fit/service problems and buying criteria before spending on traffic. Leading indicator: completed interviews with specific prior behavior and documented consent. Kill or revise the segment if fewer than a meaningful subset have a suitable use case and a viable path to supported fitting. Do not invent an eligible-case threshold after seeing the responses; predeclare it with the research design.

Create a small set of reviewed evergreen resources: when to seek urgent veterinary care, what measurements a professional needs, rear versus full support, total delivered cost, adjustment limits, printable-part qualification and repair/inspection. Proposed skills: stacks-marketing-content-strategy and stacks-marketing-seo-audit; implementation: STX views and native metadata/CMS when publishing grows. Obtain professional review for clinical content and keep source dates. Avoid mass-produced breed/diagnosis landing pages implying treatment expertise.

Potential professional referrals are the first channel experiment. Build a clear fitting packet, evidence summary, demo/loaner process and support contact before asking for referrals. These materials are drafts until evidence exists. No practice is currently a partner. Paid search/social wait until product readiness, positive first-order contribution and a defensible delivered offer are established.

## Activation: a safe next step

Today activation is educational: the visitor understands status, explores a family and sees that no money is accepted. Track this only with a privacy-reviewed analytics plan; no analytics integration is configured today. Do not relabel a planning-basket click as purchase intent or a fit approval.

At launch, activation becomes professional suitability plus complete fit information and a reviewed configuration. Proposed owner: fitting coordinator. Mechanism: reduce avoidable mismatches and returns. Measure completed/eligible fit reviews over closed windows; exclude pending cases and report their count. Native Stacks forms, models, actions, validation and notifications should implement intake and follow-up, rather than an external form database.

## Retention: service the hardware

A dog wheelchair is a durable purchase. Retention means supported use, available repair parts and responsive help, not daily app engagement. Provide approved adjustment instructions, inspection reminders where the user opts in, part compatibility and a clear escalation contact. Use native Stacks mail/notifications and queue jobs only after consent and delivery configuration exist.

Measure support resolution, part availability, repeat defect reports and return reasons. Request follow-up observations with consent. A customer still inside a fit/return observation window is pending, not a success. No lifecycle email campaign is scheduled by this project.

## Referral: earned and disclosed

After a supported fitting, invite an honest review with consent and permission for any imagery. Never require a positive review for support, warranty or a benefit. If a referral incentive is considered later, disclose it and include its cost in acquisition economics. Native commerce coupons can implement a reviewed incentive without a separate reward ledger. Current site testimonials are absent because none has been verified.

Rescue/loaner partnerships can be useful after inspection, sanitation and residual-life procedures exist. Track safe placements and servicing obligations, not vanity donation counts. Do not promise a percentage donation until economics, recipient, terms and accounting are set.

## Revenue and budget

Use the generated unit-economics model. Since repeat revenue is unproven, CAC ceilings must fit first-order contribution. The five-family portfolio should not launch simultaneously simply to create more catalog cards. A viable first family addressing the large-dog fit gap is a stronger offer than five unqualified SKUs. Select that family after measurements and professional review.

Proposed first-quarter marketing cash envelope: $1,500 total, contingent on founder approval and validation progress. Allocate $500 to reviewed educational assets, $400 to research logistics, $300 to professional demonstration materials, $150 to consented asset editing and $150 to experiments. This excludes founder time and clinical/mechanical validation, which are product-development costs. Actual approved budget is unknown, so none is committed.

A later $2,000/month experiment envelope could unlock a part-time content contractor and small acquisition tests only after measured contribution and support capacity justify it. No funding event or date is assumed. Raising capital should first unblock testing, fitting and operations; ad spend cannot compensate for an unready product.

## Ninety-day sequence and owners

| Window | Owner role | Output | Evidence needed to continue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1-14 | Founder, advisor | Interviews, claim ledger, first-family requirements | Real customer behavior and professional suitability constraints |
| Days 15-30 | Founder, technical reviewer | Reviewable education pages and fitting packet | Accurate clinical boundaries and useful user feedback |
| Days 31-60 | Product/testing lead | Bench evidence and production cost observations | Passing predeclared tests and credible BOM/labor |
| Days 61-90 | Pilot coordinator, advisor | Supervised pilot when eligible | Consent, fit outcomes and closed follow-up windows |
| After readiness | Founder, operations | Limited first-family offer | Complete validation and commerce launch gates |

Timelines depend on testing and advisory availability. The 90-day marketing plan does not override the product's gated 13-16-week development hypothesis or promise a shipping date.

## Measurement stack and experiments

| Move | Stage | Native implementation | Decision metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reviewed education | Acquisition | STX/ts-css pages; CMS when justified | Qualified visits and advisor-reviewed accuracy |
| Fit intake | Activation | Native model, migration, Action, validation and form | Completed suitable reviews, with pending counted separately |
| Fit follow-up | Retention | Native jobs/queue/mail with consent | Closed-window fit issues and time to resolution |
| Consented review request | Referral | Native notification and consent records | Honest published reviews with documented permission |
| Price/offer test | Revenue | Native catalog/commerce plus server-authoritative prices | Delivered-order contribution and return-adjusted results |

Experiments must declare audience, hypothesis, cost cap, owner, duration, success/stop criteria and how results will be interpreted before running. Avoid A/B tests on urgent medical advice or concealing product status to improve conversion. Do not install an analytics tool merely to populate a dashboard.

## Deliberately deferred

Defer influencer campaigns, affiliate recruitment, marketplace listings, wholesale, international selling, lifetime deals, broad paid advertising and subscription upsells. They multiply support or compliance work before the product has evidence. Defer programmatic SEO by breed/diagnosis because it can manufacture implied suitability. Revisit only when the binding constraint changes.

## Open decisions

Confirm budget, service region, first family, actual professional team, product evidence, support capacity, data-retention policy, consent/analytics approach and brand clearance. The highest commercial uncertainty is actual acquisition plus fit-support cost. The highest overall constraint is product readiness. Review evidence weekly; do not replace missing evidence with a more elaborate campaign calendar.
