# Buddy Wheels project documents

Prepared November 7, 2026. This is a development-stage business and product plan. No physical product has been validated, certified, manufactured or fitted in this project.

- [Unit economics](business/unit-economics.md): reproducible assumptions and scenario outputs, generated with `bun run finance`.

- [Suspension research](product/suspension-research.md): wheel isolation, damping, lockout and separately controlled rehabilitation support.
- [Actual bench CAD package](../designs/wheelchair/r0/README.md): all five families, editable CAD, printable STL parts, metal DXFs, full interface BOMs and kinematics.

- [Fabrication drawing notes](engineering/wheelchair-drawings-r0.md): datums, tube drilling, pin/spacer stacks and unresolved engineering details.

- [Buddy's configurable fit inputs](engineering/buddy-xl-measurements.md): reported 140 lb weight and explicitly estimated geometry, with a separate JSON profile.
- [Interactive wheelchair design studio](engineering/design-studio.md): native 3D inspection, bounded live dimensions, suspension views and matching download provenance.
- [Printer choices near LA](engineering/printer-options-la.md): current options, local support and starter budget.
- [Buddy Rover product brief](product/buddy-rover.md): running, beach and ski configurations, mechanical architecture and qualification gates.

- [Validation and risk register](product/validation.md): bench tests, fitting studies, traceability, failure response and launch criteria.
- [Buddy's immediate needs](product/buddy-first.md): care-first action list and the information still needed for his fit.
- [Product marketing brief](marketing/product-marketing.md): positioning, evidence, audience and voice.
- [Go-to-market plan](marketing/go-to-market.md): acquisition, activation, retention, referral, budget and experiments.
- [Research and sources](business/research.md): checked reference prices, primary sources, uncertainties and comparison limits.
- [Architecture and operations](engineering/architecture.md): native Stacks boundaries, local setup, data model and checkout gate.
- [Acceptance record](engineering/acceptance.md): executed checks, browser evidence and remaining launch work.
- [Asset provenance](marketing/assets.md): original generated imagery, prompts and intended use.
