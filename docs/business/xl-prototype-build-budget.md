## Math, tax and contingency

Base hardware = $1,427.00; equipment = $893.99; spool and power = $28.00; freight = $120.00. The tax reserve is round($2,345.99 x 9.75%) = $228.73. It includes goods, equipment and fabrication, excludes electricity/shipping and makes no statement about address-specific rates or taxable labor. Confirm actual checkout charges and location with the [CDTFA rate lookup](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/sales-use-tax-rates.htm). The 9.75% input is an illustrative LA planning rate, not a verified rate for the owner's address.

Contingency = round(20% x [hardware + spool + freight]) = round(20% x $1,572.00) = $314.40. It is a reserve, not a measured print failure rate and not a second printer charge. The total is $1,427.00 + $893.99 + $28.00 + $120.00 + $228.73 + $314.40 = **$3,012.12**. Both reserves round once to cents.

## Evidence and decision sequence

Observed on 2026-11-08: [P2S printer-only seller listing](https://additiveplus.com/product/bambu-lab-p2s/) $549; [Harbor Freight 63711 regular listing](https://www.harborfreight.com/6-in-digital-caliper-63711.html) $24.99, no membership coupon. Stock and exact checkout configuration must be confirmed. [SUNLU S2](https://www.sunlu.com/products/filadryer-s2-filament-dryer) and [Polymaker PETG](https://shop.polymaker.com/products/petg) supply capability context; no current selected-variant price was verified for this calculation. Metal fabrication, metric tube supply, wheels, shocks, softgoods and professional work all need actual quotes. No generic inch-sized component is confirmed compatible by a similar label.

1. Complete [Buddy's raw measurement worksheet](../engineering/buddy-xl-measurements.md) and professional suitability review. Keep treatment independent of the prototype.
2. Review the configured CAD, load path, shock/wheel interfaces, retention and harness anchors before requesting a fabrication quote. Provide the exact revision and BOM.
3. Obtain itemized material, finish-fit, fabrication, shipping and tax quotes. Verify the actual shock model and tune, wheel bearings/endplay and softgoods ratings. Revise the budget without changing a clinical gate to save cost.
4. Slice and calibrate only the nonstructural/fixture prints. Log actual printer, filament, grams, hours, kWh, defects and failed specimens.
5. Approve a physical test protocol before ballast or animal use. Quote the resulting redesign and validation effort separately. A successful print/export or an expensive printer does not qualify a chair.
