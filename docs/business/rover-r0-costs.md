# Buddy Rover r0 cost study

Checked 2026-11-12, USD, integer cents, native Bun arithmetic. Configuration rover-r0-l1200-w700-h950-r900-a520-g980-hub101.6-trail-swivel311. This prices the r0 bench architecture. It is not a supplier quote, a production cost or a selling price, and nothing has been bought.

## Result

At 100 carriers the modeled landed manufacturing cost is **$1,384.07 each** at listed prices. A single build at retail quantities is **$2,663.98**. The first count of this design, on November 11, was $2,037.93 and $3,254.89, with 20 inch wheels, the original cover and most prices as allowances.

The catalog's provisional Run price is $399.00, so the 30% gross-margin gate allows $279.30 and this design still misses it by $1,104.77. To pass the same gate at this cost the price would have to be about $1,978.00. The catalog price is unchanged pending the owner's decision.

If the tube is bought by weight, it drops again. $329.83 of the total is 31 lb of aluminum tube priced as 21 ft and 24 ft stock bars at $3.13 and $4.84 per foot. A 100-carrier batch is about 1.6 tons of two extrusions and would be bought from a mill or distributor. At an assumed $4.00 per pound with 8% scrap the tube line falls to $133.93 and the total to **$1,188.17**, which a price near $1,698.00 would carry. That rate is an assumption with no quote behind it.

For scale, the reference trailer sold for $729.95 at one retailer on the check date, with a 100 lb stated capacity and a 36.2 x 22.4 x 29.9 inch interior. Rover's cabin is about twice that volume. Rover does not reach that price in either scenario.

- **40 corner and 8 tee gussets, laser cut 1/8 inch 6061:** Two repeated aluminum profiles. Includes material, cutting and deburring.
- **4 stainless axle hangers:** One 304 stainless profile for all three wheels. Bore finishing after drawing review.
- **2 pneumatic trail wheels, 15 inch 4.00-8:** $32.89 each, no bulk discount assumed. Ball bearings, 5/8 inch bore and a 4 inch hub are stated by the supplier.

- **2 stock-shaft axles, sleeves, spacers, washers and cotters:** Plain 5/8 inch shaft cut and cross-drilled. No threaded journals.
- **166 sleeved bolted joints:** Zinc-plated grade 5 bolts, lock nuts and washers at Bolt Depot prices: per 1,000 for the batch, whole boxes of 100 for one build. Cut steel sleeves are an allowance.
- **Floor and ramp panels, sealed 15/32 inch ACX plywood:** 1 sheet at $52.00 plus edge and face sealing. Offcuts are charged.
- **Hinge, two latches, safety strap hardware and stand feet:** Stainless continuous hinge, two draw latches, two leveling feet with tube inserts, carriage bolts.
- **Wheel chocks, grip, tip-stop feet and floor fasteners:** Two rubber wheel chocks stand in for a parking brake during bench trials. No wheel brake is designed.
- **Printed service parts and fixtures:** About 1.3 liters of solid part volume, most of it the three ramp toes. Priced as consumed filament with a reject reserve, not a printer.
- **Cutting, drilling, deburring and assembly labor:** Batch: 5.5 h at $25/h. One-off: 15 h. Roughly 300 tube-wall holes per carrier. Timing assumptions, not observed.
- **Per-carrier inspection:** Does not replace engineering qualification.
- **Packaging:** Large flat-pack carton set. Outbound freight is separate.
- **Inbound freight allocation:** Truck freight for long bars in a batch; parcel shipping for one build. Unquoted.
- **Machine wear and shop consumables:** Drill bits, blades, jig wear.

Tube packing at 100 carriers: 242 bars of 1/16 inch wall, 21 ft each, and 147 bars of 1/8 inch wall, 24 ft each. One carrier needs 7 and 5 whole bars.

## What would bring it down further

1. A tube quote. See the mill-quantity paragraph above.
2. Joints. 166 sleeved bolted joints and the labor to drill for them come to about $212.76. Welded or riveted sub-frames, or purpose-made corner fittings, remove most of both and several kilograms. That needs a fabricator's quote.
3. Brake and front wheel. No brake is selected; the two wheelchair wheel-lock sets that were read cost $31.85 and $110.00 a pair and are made for specific chairs. A 10 inch swivel caster at $19.99 would make steering far lighter, but it stands above the deck and needs a raised front frame.
4. The cover is now $379.91. The ramp surface, floor pad and mesh are the largest material lines left; sewing is $100.00.

Selling-cost lines for the provisional price, per carrier at 100: payment fee $11.87, returns reserve $19.95, support $15.00, acquisition $35.00. Contribution before fixed overhead: -$1,066.89.
