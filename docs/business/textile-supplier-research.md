## Pack-aware costing notes

The calculations below are our arithmetic using the cited supplier units, not supplier claims. Net consumption is distinct from purchase cash and finished-set yield.

- Foam net area is 0.11102675m² per set. Area-only allocation is $2.72544/set, before nesting and offcuts. A simple feasible rectangular nest makes **10 sets per sheet**, allocating $3.15/set: rotate ten 220x350 panels to 350x220; arrange them in two columns and five rows (700x1100mm). Place eight 213x159.75 panels down the remaining right strip, and two across the bottom strip. This is an illustrative nest, not a proved optimum or a production cutting plan. The charged final shelf nest includes trim margins and 5 mm cut gaps and uses 12 sheets for 100 sets, $3.78/set. The 10-set illustration is an optimization lead, not the charged model.
- Ten mated patches mean ten hook pieces **and** ten loop pieces. The combined requirement is 0.2m of hook plus 0.2m of loop, not 0.2m total. A one-foot pair yields fifteen 20mm-long mated patches before kerf. For a 100-set batch, 20m net per side would round to 66ft before cut losses. The final model adds 1 mm between patches and respects 25ft section boundaries, requiring 70ft per side, $126.00 total or $1.26/set.
- At 100 sets, thread demand 8,000m requires nine 987.552m cones, $143.55 total, or $1.4355/set in purchase cash. Net-consumption allocation remains $1.29208/set. Test that the 80m allowance includes top thread, bobbin thread, starts/stops, and rework.
- The final cut schedule requires 8.292m/set including cover-tab edges. At 100 sets, 829.2m has a theoretical minimum of 83 ten-meter packs, but continuous panel-edge packing with 2 mm cut gaps requires 84 packs, $755.16 cash or $7.56 allocated per chair after upward-cent rounding. See the [complete cost report](xl-complete-unit-costs.md). The black-stock and binding-construction issues remain unresolved.

## Evidence limitations

Supplier product HTML and public product-variant data were read directly. Variant data resolved the 1-inch buckle price, hook/loop availability, binding color availability, and exact 5mm foam price. The Seattle double-bar product has conflicting price text. The 20mm polyester binding has a purchasable colored option but no currently purchasable black variant. These facts should remain visible in any cost model. None of the prices establish compatibility, safe working load, assembled performance, foam compression behavior, or an approved textile specification.
