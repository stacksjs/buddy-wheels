## Animal care

The [Animal Emergency & Referral Center of Minnesota](https://www.aercmn.com/blog/my-dog-cant-walk-sudden-paralysis-in-dogs/) treats sudden inability to walk or get up as an emergency and advises immediate veterinary care. This supports our urgent-care message, not a diagnosis for Buddy. Buddy's diagnosis, examination, front-leg function, pain status, measurements and care plan are unknown. Do not infer IVDD, degenerative myelopathy or any other condition.

## US regulatory discovery

The [FDA's animal-device guidance](https://www.fda.gov/animal-veterinary/animal-health-literacy/how-fda-regulates-animal-devices) explains that animal devices remain under FDA oversight despite not requiring 510(k), PMA or premarket approval. Manufacturers remain responsible for safety, effectiveness and labeling. This is a planning summary; obtain qualified advice for product classification, claims, business location, destination markets and post-sale responsibilities. Do not describe the product as FDA approved. Do not assume US rules apply elsewhere.
