**Every row loses money.** The Run COGS is the counted cost of the r0 bench design at 100 units from the [r0 cost study](rover-r0-costs.md), which is higher than the price. Coast, Snow and All-season add the earlier target steps of $35, $75 and $135 for conversion kits that have not been designed. A 30% gross margin at $999 needs the cost at $699.30 or less; the cost study lists what it would take to get there.

Fees assume 2.9% plus $0.30; reserve assumes 5% of product revenue. Tax collected is not revenue. Freight, acquisition and support are management assumptions, not quotes, and the freight figures predate the mass estimate: they are low for a carrier of about 110 lb. The starting design is now a welded frame that ships assembled by truck, which these freight figures do not cover. Returns, dimensional freight and multi-box handling require actual quotes before purchasing inventory.

These contribution figures exclude fixed overhead and development costs. They do not change the wheelchair-only demand scenarios. Do not open orders at these prices until the counted cost is below them.
