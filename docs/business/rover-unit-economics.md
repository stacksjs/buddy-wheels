Fees assume 2.9% plus $0.30; reserve assumes 5% of product revenue. Tax collected is not revenue. COGS must cover the chassis, cabin, running gear, all included conversion parts, qualified printing, rejects, assembly, QA and packaging. Freight is a cost assumption, not a customer delivery quote. Acquisition is $35 per first order. Returns, dimensional freight and multi-box handling require actual quotes before purchasing inventory.

These contribution figures exclude fixed overhead and development costs. They do not change the wheelchair-only demand scenarios or prove profitability. Shared overhead must be allocated once across the actual portfolio, not charged twice or omitted. No conversion-kit repeat revenue is assumed. Quote and validate the Run chassis first, then quote each incremental kit separately. If the tested design cannot meet these envelopes, revise the offer and price before opening orders.

The [r0 cost study](rover-r0-costs.md) counts an actual bill of materials for the first bench design and does not fit the Run row above. The COGS column here is a target, not a finding. Treat every contribution figure in this table as unsupported until the architecture or the price changes.
