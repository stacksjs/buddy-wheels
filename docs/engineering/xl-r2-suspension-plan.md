# XL r2 standard die-spring cartridge

Scope: answer the request for a cheaper standard-parts adjustable suspension, checked online, with matching CAD, studio, BOM, assembly instructions and cost documents. Preserve the published r0 and rigid r1 designs.

## Selected architecture

Two MISUMI SWH20-75 compression springs in independent, captive vertical carriages. Standard 1 inch square aluminum carriages slide in 1.25 inch housings with replaceable UHMW liners. Laser-cut steel seats and shims supplement the existing repeated T gussets and axle patches. Smooth, positively retained pins capture the spring seat and carriage travel. No printed primary load path.

Adjustment is 0/1/2 mm preload by equal steel shims per side. Nominal stroke is 8 mm. Spring rate is fixed at 65.3 N/mm and there is no damper. Do not call preload a stiffness adjustment. Manual preview travel is geometry only. The unpriced second spring-rate candidate was excluded rather than presenting an assumed price as verified.

This is a bench design. Buddy's anatomy, actual wheel hub length, complete textile and guard patterns, load capacity, spring tune, friction/rebound, fatigue and stability remain unqualified. No animal use or terrain/training claim follows from digital exports.

XLSpring owns r2 configuration, datums and BOM. XLSpringCost owns integer-cent scenarios. The CAD reuses a frozen r1 geometry dependency, with a separate r2 source hash. The native Buddy export command produces actual STL/DXF/PNG outputs and mesh inspections. The native STX ModelViewer inspects actual part meshes and nominal assembly geometry, with a cutaway for the cartridge.
