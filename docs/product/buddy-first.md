# Buddy comes first

Buddy has stopped using his hind legs. He is very large, and the founder found that few available chairs offered a suitable size. Fit availability and affordability both motivate this project. We do not know the cause, timing, pain level, veterinary findings or treatment plan. This document does not diagnose him.

If this is new or sudden, call a veterinarian or emergency clinic now and follow their transport instructions. The [veterinary emergency guidance](https://www.aercmn.com/blog/my-dog-cant-walk-sudden-paralysis-in-dogs/) treats sudden inability to walk as an emergency. Do not delay assessment to print, assemble, photograph or measure a chair.

## Immediate parallel path

1. Establish the veterinary care plan and whether any mobility aid is appropriate now.
2. Ask about an available commercially produced cart, a rehabilitation practice's loaner, rental or rescue equipment bank. Availability must be checked locally.
3. Ask the professional to guide support during transport, toileting and measurement. Do not force standing or manipulate painful limbs for this project.
4. Record the constraints that matter to Buddy before selecting geometry. His needs can inform requirements without making him an uncontrolled prototype test.

## Information still needed

- Body weight, breed/body shape, age and relevant growth expectations.
- Approximate onset and whether a professional has assessed it.
- Professional judgment about front-leg strength, posture and suitability of rear versus full support.
- Rear-leg height, body length, body width and harness clearances, taken as the professional directs.
- Skin/contact concerns, fatigue signs and the permitted activity/rest plan.
- Home terrain, doorway width, transport and caregiver handling limitations.

The fit planner currently gives only a proposed family to discuss. It does not confirm fit, approve weight capacity, select a treatment or establish that Buddy may use a chair. No individualized dimensions or safe-use schedule can be supplied from the information currently available.
