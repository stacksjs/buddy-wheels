This is what one builder buys for one bench carrier, in whole bars, sheets, yards and packs, so it is more than the material the carrier consumes. It is a bench build. ## Ways to lower this total

Each line is a choice with a real price behind it. None is applied above.

| Choice | Saves about | What you give up |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| The same front caster without a swivel lock, from its maker | $23.31 | The lock that holds the wheel straight for running |
| Leave out the clear storm panels | $89.35 | Wind and rain protection at the windows. They can be added later |
| Rigid rear axle instead of sprung wheels | $119.34 | The suspension |
| Not taken: plates in 5052-H32 instead of 6061-T6 | $167.38 | Strength. 5052-H32 is roughly 30% weaker and the plates hold every joint. The owner chose to keep 6061-T6 on 2026-11-12; the price is recorded only |

## Before you order

1. Measure the dog and set the cabin size first. Every tube cut and fabric blank follows from it.
2. Buy one wheel and measure its hub before cutting an axle or ordering hangers.
3. Print the clearance coupon and one cap, then the rest.
4. Read the release gates in assembly.md. This list builds a bench carrier for fit and handling trials with ballast, not a finished product.
