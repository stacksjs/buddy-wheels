# Buddy Rover r0 buy list for one carrier

Configuration rover-r0-l1300-w700-h1000-r1000-a560-g1000-hub101.6-trail-swivel310-k350-t180-welded. Checked 2026-11-12, USD, before tax and shipping. This is what one builder buys for one bench carrier, in whole bars, sheets, yards and packs, so it is more than the material the carrier consumes. It is a bench build. Nothing here is rated or approved for carrying an animal.

**Materials to buy: about $2,255.42.** $1,493.42 of that has a supplier page behind it. The remaining $762.00 is allowance and needs a quote or a shop visit. Your own time is extra: the study assumes about 6 hours of cutting and assembly, with the welding done by a shop, and 9 hours of sewing.

## Before you order

1. Measure the dog and set the cabin size first. Every tube cut and fabric blank follows from it.
2. Buy one wheel and measure its hub before cutting an axle or ordering hangers.
3. Print the clearance coupon and one cap, then the rest.
4. Read the release gates in assembly.md. This list builds a bench carrier for fit and handling trials with ballast, not a finished product.
